A professional app for the news organisation's reporters and accredited field crews, not a consumer app. Using it requires a professional account issued by the organisation's administration.

Core functions:
- Send original field material: upload video and photo files at their original quality from the photo library, without compression or re-encoding, together with the fixed fields and the desk form.
- Light copy first: the app generates a light copy that reaches the newsroom before the original, which finishes uploading in the background.
- Automatic resume: uploads use a resumable protocol and resume from the last confirmed chunk when the connection returns or the app is opened again.
- Numbered receipt: the app computes the file's checksum on the phone and the server verifies it to ensure the material arrived complete, then shows a receipt number that can be shared inside the organisation.
- Three independent states per material: technical, editorial and rights, with a timeline of who did what and when, plus supervisor notes.
- Local copy and emergencies: deleting the local copy after the receipt is the reporter's decision through the system confirmation, with an emergency wipe of app data and an automatic wipe if the session is revoked remotely.

Data transparency and privacy (what is uploaded and who sees it):
- What is uploaded: video and photo files the reporter chooses manually, the fixed fields and desk form attached to them, plus the device integrity token (App Attest) and the notification token for newsroom alerts about material status; these two pass through Apple services. The app itself does not request location permission, does not send persistent hardware identifiers, and does not track users across other apps or websites.
- The original is uploaded as is: without compression or re-encoding to preserve its checksum, so its metadata may carry what the camera recorded. The original is stored on the organisation's servers and is never shown in raw form; this data is stripped from every preview and delivered copy.
- Who sees the material: material is processed on the organisation's servers and reviewed by authorised editors and reviewers according to role permissions and source-protection rules. Material flagged as sensitive never leaves to any external provider; other material may pass through an external processing provider under the organisation's rules. Subject to its rights and by the organisation's decision, material may be licensed to purchasing media organisations, which receive copies stripped of revealing data; the raw original is never delivered. Data is not used for advertising.
