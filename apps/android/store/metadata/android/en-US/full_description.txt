A professional field-ingest application built exclusively for accredited newsroom reporters and field crews. This app is not intended for the general public and requires an organization-issued account to sign in.

Key capabilities:
- Original field footage submission: attach original, uncompressed video, audio and photo files directly from the device picker alongside the newsroom's structured metadata form.
- Reliable resumable uploads: powered by the tus resumable upload protocol, transfers resume from the exact last confirmed byte after network drops, app restarts or device reboots.
- End-to-end integrity verification: a local SHA-256 digest is computed on the device and verified by the server before an official ingest receipt number is issued.
- Local copy control and emergency wipe: reporters decide when to remove local media copies via a system confirmation after receiving a server receipt; includes a manual emergency session wipe and automatic local wipe on remote session revocation.

Data collection and visibility (what is uploaded and who sees it):
- What is uploaded: only the media files the reporter explicitly selects and the accompanying editorial form fields (headline, summary, reported event date and place as entered in the form, rights notes), plus a Play Integrity attestation token and a push notification token for receipt delivery; these two pass through Google services. The app itself does not request location permission and does not transmit persistent hardware identifiers.
- The original file is uploaded as recorded: without compression or re-encoding, to preserve its digest, so its metadata may contain the location, time and device model the camera embedded. The original is stored on the organization's servers, is never shown to anyone in raw form, and this metadata is stripped from every preview and every delivered copy.
- Who sees the material: media and editorial forms go to the organization's own Visual Library servers and are seen by authorized newsroom editors and reviewers under role-based access control and source-protection policies. Under its rights and by the organization's decision, material may be licensed to buyer media organizations, which receive copies stripped of revealing metadata; the raw original is never delivered. Data is not used for advertising.
