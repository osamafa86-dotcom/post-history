An internal app for the visual library's reporters and crew, not for the public; sign-in uses an account issued by the office.

- New material: the required fields and the office form, with the original file from the phone's gallery, uncompressed.
- Uploads resume from the last byte received after a network loss, the app being closed or the phone restarting.
- The file's fingerprint is computed on the phone and checked by the server, then a receipt number arrives.
- Deleting the local copy after the receipt is the reporter's decision.
- Emergency wipe of the app's data, and automatic wipe when the session is revoked remotely.

Uploading needs the app installed from Google Play and Google Play services.
