A professional field-ingest application built exclusively for accredited newsroom reporters and field crews. This app is not intended for the general public and requires an organization-issued account to sign in.

Key capabilities:
- Original field footage submission: attach original, uncompressed video, audio and photo files directly from the device picker alongside the newsroom's structured metadata form.
- Reliable resumable uploads: powered by the tus resumable upload protocol, transfers resume from the exact last confirmed byte after network drops, app restarts or device reboots.
- End-to-end integrity verification: a local SHA-256 digest is computed on the device and verified by the server before an official ingest receipt number is issued.
- Local copy control and emergency wipe: reporters decide when to remove local media copies via a system confirmation after receiving a server receipt; includes a manual emergency session wipe and automatic local wipe on remote session revocation.

Data collection and visibility (what is uploaded and who sees it):
- What is uploaded: only the media files the reporter explicitly selects and the accompanying editorial form fields (headline, summary, reported event date and place as entered in the form, rights notes), plus a Play Integrity attestation token and a push notification token for receipt delivery. The app does not collect background GPS location and does not transmit persistent hardware identifiers.
- Who sees it: uploaded media and editorial forms go exclusively to the organization's own Visual Library servers. Access is restricted to authorized newsroom editors, reviewers and archive administrators under role-based access control and source-protection policies. No data is shared with advertisers or unauthorized third parties.
