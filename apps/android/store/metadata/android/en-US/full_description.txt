A professional field-ingest application built exclusively for accredited newsroom reporters and field crews. This app is not intended for the general public and requires an organization-issued account to sign in.

Key capabilities:
- Original field footage submission: attach original, uncompressed video and photo files from the phone's gallery alongside the fixed fields and the office form.
- Reliable resumable uploads: powered by the tus resumable upload protocol, transfers resume from the exact last confirmed byte after network drops, app restarts or device reboots.
- Integrity check: a SHA-256 digest is computed on the phone and verified by the server so the material arrives complete and intact; the receipt number is then shown inside the app.
- Local copy control and emergency wipe: deleting the local copy after the receipt is the reporter's decision via a system confirmation; includes an emergency wipe of the app's data and an automatic wipe when the session is revoked remotely.

Data collection and visibility (what is uploaded and who sees it):
- What is uploaded: only the video and photo files the reporter explicitly selects, with the fixed fields and the office form, plus a Play Integrity attestation token and a push notification token for newsroom alerts about the material's status; these two pass through Google services. The app itself does not request location permission and does not transmit persistent hardware identifiers.
- The original file is uploaded as recorded: without compression or re-encoding, to preserve its digest, so its metadata may contain the location, time and device model the camera embedded. The original is stored on the organization's servers, is never shown to anyone in raw form, and this metadata is stripped from every preview and every delivered copy.
- Who sees the material: material is processed on the organization's servers and seen by authorized newsroom editors and reviewers under role-based access control and source-protection policies. Material flagged as sensitive never leaves to any external provider; other material may pass through an external processing provider under the organization's rules. Under its rights and by the organization's decision, material may be licensed to buyer media organizations, which receive copies stripped of revealing metadata; the raw original is never delivered. Data is not used for advertising.

Uploading needs the app installed from Google Play and Google Play services.
